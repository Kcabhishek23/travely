No more scattered blog posts, unanswered messages, or guides you can't verify.

01

Find routes that match your pace

Filter treks by difficulty, altitude, and season instead of guessing from photos. Every route on Traveler is mapped from real GPS tracks submitted by past trekkers and local guides.

  • Difficulty ratings from people who actually walked the trail
  • Real elevation and distance data, not marketing estimates
  • Seasonal warnings for weather and trail closures
02

Book guides you can actually trust

Every guide on Traveler is verified against Nepal's official trekking guide license registry. You see their certification, languages spoken, and reviews from previous trips before you pay anything.

  • License and insurance checked before a guide can list a trip
  • Direct messaging to ask questions before booking
  • Ratings tied to verified completed trips only
03

One itinerary, not five different apps

Your permits, guide contact, daily route, and payment sit in a single itinerary you can open offline — useful when you're in a valley with no signal for three days.

  • Offline access to your itinerary and downloaded maps
  • Automatic permit reminders (TIMS, ACAP, and others)
  • One booking record instead of scattered emails
About Us

Built by people who got lost in Mustang first

Traveler started in 2022 after our founders spent two days trying to confirm a guide booking over email for a trek that started the next morning. We built the platform we wished had existed: a direct line between trekkers and licensed local guides, with no middleman agency marking up the price.

Today Traveler works with guide cooperatives across Mustang, Annapurna, Langtang, and the Everest region — keeping more of what you pay with the people actually leading your trek.
